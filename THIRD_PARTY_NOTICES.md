Copyright 2015 The Google Sans Flex Authors
(https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans-flex).

The unmodified variable font in `app/src/main/assets/fonts/google_sans_flex.ttf`
is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, not the project's MIT license.
The complete notice and license are included beside the font in
`app/src/main/assets/fonts/OFL-GoogleSansFlex.txt` and bundled in the APK.
The runtime selects its weight and roundness axes; the font file is unmodified.

The local Marin wallpaper samples are separate test downloads. Their source
notices are written to `.tooling/test-wallpapers/SOURCES.txt`; they are not
bundled or included under the project's license.
