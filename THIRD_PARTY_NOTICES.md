## Google Sans Flex

Copyright 2015 The Google Sans Flex Authors
(https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans-flex).

The unmodified variable font in `app/src/main/assets/fonts/google_sans_flex.ttf`
is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, not the project's MIT license.
The complete notice and license are included beside the font in
`app/src/main/assets/fonts/OFL-GoogleSansFlex.txt` and bundled in the APK.
The runtime selects its weight and roundness axes; the font file is unmodified.

## Bundled wallpaper test pack

Twelve original images are bundled in `app/src/main/assets/wallpapers` for visual
and palette testing: My Dress-Up Darling, Roshidere, Bunny Girl Senpai, and six
user-provided images from their local wallpaper collection. These images are
excluded from the source code's MIT license. Character/artwork rights remain
with their respective rights holders; inclusion does not relicense the artwork.

The complete per-image source list and SHA-256 hashes are included beside the
images in `app/src/main/assets/wallpapers/SOURCES.json`, which is also packaged
in the APK. Sources for user-provided images identify the original local filename;
creator attribution was not available for those files.
